# Automated QA scope

The records document operationally blinded automated semantic solving, counterexample/design checks, and routed adjudication in separate reviewer contexts. Where initial judgments were sealed, generator/oracle comparisons occurred afterward and were not treated as authority over rendered-text judgments.

Shared model backends, shared filesystem access, incomplete tool traces, and unproven technical file isolation remain limitations. These records do not establish independent human annotation or backend-independent review. Status labels such as PASS_LIMITED apply to those operational procedures, not to universal semantic validity or novelty.

The original accepted archive has incomplete rejection provenance. The broader development repair followed post-score semantic/design findings and is explicitly exploratory. The parser method remains inactive on its main wording batch; zero observed harm during fallback does not estimate harm when a repair actually activates.

No new reviewer, adjudicator, model response, acceptance estimate, or synthetic human acceptance score was produced during this release preparation. Excluded transport/conversation traces and planning archives remain in the original local project; they were not deleted. Release evidence is sufficient to reproduce the paper's stored-score analysis, with the stated limits on auditing original QA and selection.
