# AI assistance and automated QA

AI assistance was used in research construction, implementation, analysis support, review coordination, and manuscript editing. The archive preserves model contracts, review records that were selected for export, and material correction history. This edition performs only packaging and deterministic offline replay.

Automated reviewer roles used operationally separated contexts with shared backends, shared filesystem infrastructure, and incomplete tool/isolation traces. This does not demonstrate independent human annotation or strong access isolation. Original accepted-item selection has incomplete rejected-candidate provenance. Evaluation models are distinct roles from automated reviewers; they do not establish annotation validity by voting.

Local raw scores and the earlier three-provider final-answer records are scientific observations. Hosted records concern the original 48-query boundary cohort only; there are no hosted ALIGN3 scores. Thinking blocks, signatures, credentials, and unrelated account envelopes are omitted from exported provider records. These omissions and path/hash adaptations are documented in `provenance/SANITIZATION_RULES.md`.

No new human-review count, reviewer independence certification, novelty judgment, or venue-policy compliance claim is made. The exact supplied eight-page manuscript is unchanged. Any later public announcement or venue submission must describe assistance consistently with the applicable policy.
